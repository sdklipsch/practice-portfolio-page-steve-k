Why LaunchCode?

As I mentioned before, I have been interesting in learning how to code and becoming a software engineer for a long time. I always had difficulty trying to focus on any online classes and didn't have structure to force myself to continue when I got bogged down with work. Working at Nativo and getting exposed to code I realized how little I know. I mainly just copied existing code, test templates or ask claude without any real understanding of what was going on. I had zero confidence in my abilities becuase to me I didn't really do anything. When I was searching for a new job I felt stuck because I wasn't really a full SDET because I couldn't really build good smart code. Also now that I have to try to build from scratch I am just asking claude to do everything. I don't want to be a person who asked AI to do coding but doesnt understand it. for a few reasons

  • I want to be able to give concise direction
  • I want to be able to diagnose issue myself
  • I want to be able to code
  • I want to be a software engineer

That lead my to LaunchCode. I have an Aunt who graduated a few years ago and she has been telling me ever since that I should take it. My issue was always time. So when I lost my job and my new job has way better hours, I knew this was my time to act. I am excited about what we have learned so far and what we will continue to learn in the future
